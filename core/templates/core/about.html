Why PESU Nexus?

As a student at PES University, I noticed that preparing for exams often meant jumping between different places to find what was needed.

Relevant notes, question banks, and other academic resources are frequently scattered across WhatsApp groups, Google Drive links, and different student communities.

At the same time, there wasn't a single platform focused on timed mock tests where students could practice under exam-like conditions and see their performance relative to other students through dynamic leaderboards.

PESU Nexus was built to solve that problem: one platform, built for students, by a student.

The problem

PROBLEM / 01

Scattered Resources

Academic materials can be spread across WhatsApp conversations, Drive folders, and various links, making it difficult to find the right resource when you need it.

PROBLEM / 02

Limited Exam Practice

Students need a convenient way to take timed mock tests that recreate the time pressure and structure of an actual examination.

PROBLEM / 03

No Central Performance View

A score by itself doesn't always provide context. Dynamic leaderboards can help students understand their relative performance after a mock test.

PROBLEM / 04

Disconnected Tools

When preparation resources and testing tools exist separately, students have to switch between multiple platforms during their preparation.

What PESU Nexus brings together

⏱

Timed Mock Tests

Take practice tests with time limits and get a more exam-like preparation experience.

◈

Dynamic Leaderboards

Compare your mock-test performance with other students through dynamically updated rankings.

▤

Academic Materials

Bring useful academic resources into one accessible place instead of searching through scattered links.
